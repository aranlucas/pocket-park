# Pocket Park

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript and Vite keep the local-first prototype small and reviewable.

## Users

People interested in short tactile physics games using desktop or mobile browsers. This audience is a design assumption, not a validated market claim.

## Product Purpose

Make a small change to a launch, understand what it changed, and enjoy finding a better line. The game is an abstract puck puzzle, not a realistic skating simulator.

## Operating Context

Short sessions on desktop or mobile, without accounts or an external service. Scores stay on the device. Hints deliberately reveal a reference solution rather than an AI-generated answer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep the art faithful to simulated geometry and the preview faithful to the launch. The violet/chalk/coral palette and rounded heavy serif silhouette are intentional; Fraunces was chosen for fidelity to the original concept. Mint marks success and progression. Pair visual state with labeled controls, focus indicators and status text.

No network leaderboard, analytics, audio, gamepad support or replay export is part of the current prototype. Future priorities and acceptance criteria are in the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## Evidence on Hand

Synthetic parks and automated tests; no customer research or performance benchmarks.

## Product Principles

- Make the launch interaction immediately available.
- Keep user data on the device.
- Show simulation, storage and offline-support boundaries honestly.
- Preserve undo and visible recovery.
